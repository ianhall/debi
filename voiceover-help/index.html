Voice-Over 101

Want to use your voice to make money?

Learn the ins and outs of how to get started, including: microphone etiquette, how to make some of the most common noises and sounds (you might be surprised), working with copy, creating a demo reel, what to expect in the studio, the business of the business and much more.

This is, hands down, the most comprehensive book on Voice-Over available.

From beginners to pros, you’ll find value in Debi’s wealth of knowledge. Her tips and advice are the gems that only a true industry veteran and hard-working professional would know. Now she’s sharing it all with you!
