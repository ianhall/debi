Biography

Debi Derryberry is best known as the voice of Jimmy Neutron in the Academy Award-nominated film Jimmy Neutron: Boy Genius and the Emmy Award-winning TV series. Her extensive voice acting credits include Draculaura (Monster High), Ms. Alegra (The Loud House), along with roles in F Is for Family, Sharkdog, Bugs Bunny Builders, Pokémon Horizons, and hundreds of other animated productions.

Her video game credits include The Sims, CookieRun: Kingdom, Crash Bandicoot, God of War, Final Fantasy, Guild Wars 2, Genshin Impact, and World of Warcraft. Anime fans know her from Rising Impact, Zatch Bell!, Sailor Moon, and Glitter Force. Feature film credits include Toy Story, The Boy and the Heron, Despicable Me 2, Kiki’s Delivery Service, and The Christmas Chronicles.


Debi has built a social media audience of over 1.3 million TikTok followers with more than 28 million likes.


In addition to her performing career, Debi is a respected voice-over coach. She was voted Best Voice-Over Coach by Backstage West and is the author of Voice Over 101: How to Succeed as a Voice Actor, available on Amazon.


A passionate children’s entertainer, Debi has written and recorded five award-winning children’s music albums. Her songs are available on all major streaming platforms, including her recent releases Go to Sleep and Gotta Go Green. Her single “Baby Banana” reached #1 on SiriusXM Kids Place Live, and she recently teamed up with Grammy Award winner Lisa Loeb on the song “Compost Bin.”


Debi also creates animated music videos on her YouTube channel, Debi Derryberry KIDS, where families can enjoy fun, educational songs for young children:


youtube.com/@DebiDerryberryKIDS

  • Wednesday from “The Addams Family“

  • Clay on “Playhouse Disney“

Also an on-camera actress, Debi's credits include many commercials, "iCarly" and "True Beauty", and she can be seen in the features "Ghostworld", "Comic Book the Movie" and "Free Willy". In that film, Debi was the stunt double who actually rode the whale!

When she was only nine years old, she got her first guitar and wrote My Dog's My Buddy which is featured on her first CD, What a Way to Play!.

Debi’s 4th and newest kids album, Gotta Go Green has just received the prestigious NAPPA Award (National Parenting Products award).

Her previous 3 albums are What a Way To Play, Very Derryberry, and Baby Banana!. 
Baby Banana, the title song from her third CD went to number one on the charts for SiriusXM Radio!

Debi Derryberry is also the author of  the book Baby Banana and The Licorice Tree which goes with the CD.

All her products can be found online through all streaming platforms (Spotify, Apple, Amazon, Pandora, etc!)

Debi was born and raised in the quiet desert community of Indio, California, reaping the benefits of a close and loving family who encouraged her to pursue her dreams. 

Debi excelled in high school, serving as captain of the gymnastics team and on the student council. She graduated from UCLA as a pre-med candidate, and then seeking to explore her creative side, moved to Nashville, Tennessee. After three years in Music City, she returned to Los Angeles to launch her voiceover career.

Since then, she has played feature roles in many high-profile films, TV shows, cartoons, video games and more.
